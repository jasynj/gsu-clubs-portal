Office Mission

The Office of Student Engagement and Leadership (SEAL) is the coordinating unit for all organizations at Grambling State University. The office is located in Favrot Student Union, Office #218, and can be reached at (318) 274-3334.

SEAL encourages students to get involved through organizations to learn civic responsibility, activism, and how to make a difference in the lives of others.

What Is a Student Organization?

A student organization is a group of currently enrolled GSU students who unite to promote a common interest.

Only currently enrolled undergraduate students may serve as officers or vote on organizational matters. Recognition as a GSU student organization is not an endorsement by GSU of an organization's purpose or activities.

Requirements to Maintain Status

Use the tabs to view requirements for Clubs & Organizations or NPHC Greeks.

NPHC Greek Requirements

  • Must meet all Clubs & Organizations requirements listed in the other tab.
  • Must have 30 completed non-remedial credit hours at GSU and be classified as sophomore or above.
  • Must have at least a 2.5 cumulative GPA to apply for membership.
  • Must have been enrolled full-time and completed the prior semester at GSU.
  • Must be in good standing with no disciplinary sanctions within the past two years.
  • Must be cleared by SEAL.
  • Chapter officers must be financially active at chapter, regional, and national levels.
  • Must be an active member of the Pan-Hellenic Council.
  • Must have three designated delegates to attend PHC Council meetings.
  • NPHC monthly meetings are separate from general organization monthly meetings.
  • Intake occurs twice a year (October for fall, February for spring); chapters must select one cycle, not both.
  • Must be in good standing with the chapter's national office.
  • Members from other chapters or universities are not recognized and must be excluded from GSU chapter activity.
  • Students may not be initiated into or join Greek organizations at another university.
  • All intake activities must be held on the GSU campus.

Clubs & Organizations Requirements

  • Must register with SEAL every fall and spring semester.
  • Must maintain at least 10 members including officers.
  • All members must be enrolled full-time (12 credit hours).
  • Must have an advisor who is a full-time GSU faculty or staff member.
  • Members must maintain a 2.0 cumulative GPA.
  • Officers must maintain a 2.5 cumulative GPA.
  • Must have a constitution and bylaws on file with SEAL.
  • Must have an agency account with the GSU finance office.
  • Must attend SEAL orientation/registration each semester.
  • Must attend monthly SEAL meetings (fines apply for missing meetings).
  • Must complete two community service projects per semester plus SEAL-selected University service projects.
  • Must participate in Club and Organization Day.
  • Must participate in Miss GSU Coronation with a queen and escort.
  • Must submit meeting minutes and attendance within two days of each meeting.
  • Must attend Leadership Seminars.
  • Must deposit all organization funds in a University account.
  • Must submit the Annual Officer Registration transition form between April 15 and May 15.
  • Must complete the Anti-hazing course on CANVAS.
  • All members must sign a statement of understanding regarding the hazing policy.

How to Start a New Organization

Complete each step before chartering and official recognition.

1

Inform SEAL and Build Founding Group

Notify SEAL that you are proposing a new group. Gather at least 10 founding members who are full-time students and meet the minimum 2.0 GPA requirement.

2

Hold Three Pre-Charter Meetings

Reserve campus space and hold three meetings before chartering. During this period, business is limited to drafting the constitution and forming your organization structure.

3

Prepare Required Documents

Draft the constitution using SEAL's sample format. Designate a president, treasurer, and other officers on the New Student Organization Application (NSOA). Secure a full-time faculty/staff advisor and advisor agreement form.

4

Submit for Review

Submit all documents to SEAL. Nationally affiliated groups must include a letter authorizing campus colonization and a letter of advisor support.

5

Finalize Recognition Requirements

Recognition is granted the following semester after approval. The proposed organization must not duplicate an existing organization, and all founding members must complete the Anti-hazing course on CANVAS.

Registration Will Be Denied If

  • The organization is not formed for an educational purpose.
  • Requirements are incomplete or contain false material.
  • There are fewer than 10 committed students.
  • The organization duplicates a currently registered organization.
  • The organization is under sanction.
  • The group attempts to re-register under a new name while under sanction.
  • The organization is under suspension from its off-campus affiliate.
  • The organization has delinquent debts.

Organization Categories at GSU

Organizations may be recognized under one or more of the following categories.

  • Honor
  • Political
  • Professional/Academic/Honorary
  • Religious
  • Service/Social/Special Interest
  • Health and Wellness
  • Media & Publications
  • Sports Clubs
  • University Established (SGA, Student Union Board, Band, Cheerleaders, etc.)
  • NPHC Greek organizations

Constitution Requirements

Every student organization constitution must include the following.

  • Organization name
  • Purpose and goals
  • Membership eligibility and requirements
  • Officer disciplinary procedures (notice and perspective)
  • Member disciplinary procedures (notice and perspective)
  • Officer selection process (timing and process)
  • Officer duties for every position
  • GPA and student status requirements
  • Financial statement: "All monies belonging to this organization shall be deposited and disbursed through a bank account established for this organization in the Office of the Comptroller at GSU."
  • Procedure to amend the constitution
  • Dissolution clause (remaining funds/equipment transfer to SEAL)
