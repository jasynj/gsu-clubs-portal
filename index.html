Our Mission

The Office of Clubs and Organizations at Grambling State University is dedicated to fostering a vibrant campus community by supporting and empowering student-led organizations. We provide the resources, guidance, and framework necessary for students to develop leadership skills, build lasting connections, and create meaningful experiences outside the classroom.

Our mission is to ensure that every student has the opportunity to find their place within the Grambling community, whether through Greek-letter organizations, academic clubs, service groups, or special interest organizations. We believe that involvement in student organizations is a vital part of the college experience that cultivates personal growth, professional development, and a lifelong commitment to service.

Requirements

Maintain your organization's active status by meeting the following requirements.

Greek Organization Requirements

  • GPA Requirement: Maintain a cumulative GPA of 2.75 or higher (individual members and chapter average).
  • Advisor: Must have at least one faculty or staff advisor approved by the university.
  • National Affiliation: Must maintain good standing with the national/international governing body.
  • Membership Intake: All intake processes must comply with university anti-hazing policies and be approved by the Office of Greek Life.
  • Community Service: Complete a minimum of 20 community service hours per semester as a chapter.
  • Registration: Re-register with the Office of Clubs & Organizations at the beginning of each academic year.
  • Meetings: Attend all mandatory Greek Council meetings and university-sponsored Greek events.
  • Financial Obligations: Maintain all financial obligations with the university and national organization.
  • Constitution: Submit an up-to-date constitution and bylaws on file with the Office of Clubs & Organizations.
  • Risk Management: Complete annual risk management and anti-hazing training for all members.

Club & Organization Requirements

  • GPA Requirement: The president and officers must maintain a cumulative GPA of 2.5 or higher.
  • Advisor: Must have at least one faculty or staff advisor approved by the university.
  • Minimum Membership: Maintain a minimum of 5 active members at all times.
  • Registration: Re-register with the Office of Clubs & Organizations at the beginning of each academic year.
  • Meetings: Hold a minimum of 2 general body meetings per month during the academic year.
  • Community Service: Complete a minimum of 10 community service hours per semester as an organization.
  • Events: Host or participate in at least 2 campus events per semester.
  • Constitution: Submit an up-to-date constitution and bylaws on file with the Office of Clubs & Organizations.
  • Financial Report: Submit a financial report at the end of each semester if the organization manages funds.
  • Officer Transition: Complete an officer transition report when leadership changes occur.

How to Start a Club

Follow these steps to establish a new student organization at Grambling State University.

1

Gather Interest

Recruit at least 5 interested students who share your vision. Identify a faculty or staff member willing to serve as your advisor.

2

Draft a Constitution

Write a constitution and bylaws outlining your organization's purpose, membership criteria, officer roles, and meeting schedule.

3

Submit Application

Complete the New Organization Application form and submit it along with your constitution to the Office of Clubs & Organizations.

4

Attend Orientation

Once approved, attend the mandatory New Organization Orientation session to learn about university policies and resources available to you.

5

Get Started

Begin holding meetings, planning events, and building your organization's presence on campus. The Office of Clubs & Organizations is here to support you.
